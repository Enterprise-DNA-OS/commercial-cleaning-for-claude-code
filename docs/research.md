# Why this target

Picked on 27 October 2026 as a new family for the Enterprise DNA rebuild series: commercial cleaning contractors.

## The bill

Swept prices by location, in bands ([sweptworks.com/plan-pricing](https://sweptworks.com/plan-pricing), checked 27 October 2026; the page shows "$" and does not name the currency; the 50-location figures are in the page's price data, shown by its location selector):

| Plan | Up to 15 locations | Up to 50 locations |
|---|---|---|
| Optimize | $150 a month, or $1,440 a year billed yearly | $350 a month, or $3,360 a year |
| Scale | $225 a month, or $2,160 a year billed yearly | $500 a month, or $4,800 a year |
| Accelerate | "Let's talk": custom pricing, no cap on locations, for 50 or more | |

The API ("Swept Connect: API, Webhooks, and MCP") is an add-on with no published price. Above 50 locations, "the pricing and the rollout both change". Most cleaning contractors run payroll, rostering and inspection apps beside it.

## Why it fits

- **Data-shaped.** Clients, sites, scopes of works, cleaners, visits, inspections and faults, periodicals, complaints, keys, invoices. No real-time collaboration needed to run the business.
- **The rituals are weekly and repeatable:** who covers tonight's gap, which periodicals are late, which site failed its inspection, which site loses money, what to invoice.
- **The rules are specific:** the Cleaning Services Award's minimum engagement by floor area, award rates, and New Zealand's Part 6A right to transfer when a contract changes hands.
- **What reviewers say.** Capterra reviews mention the app crashing or being slow, geofencing that "doesn't always work", no automatic clock-out when staff forget, and building spreadsheets to finish data work.

## What stays with a custom build

GPS clock-in on a phone, photos on inspection items, a client portal. Enterprise DNA builds those into a business's own version where they matter.
